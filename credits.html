Emulator credits & source

NES Dream Player uses the open-source EmulatorJS 4.2.3 frontend and its Nestopia NES core. The player is not affiliated with Nintendo, Dragon Ball, Delta, or their owners.

  • EmulatorJS NES core source and build scripts

The matching Contra Power profile uses JSNES 2.1.0 (Apache-2.0), with custom timing and projectile rendering. JSNES source · Licence · Timing source · Player source. All bundled core sources are served under /power-core/src/.

The owner-requested modified Contra, DBZ II and Captain Tsubasa II games are available from the quick-play library. Additional imported ROMs and all saves remain in the browser’s device storage. Clearing site data can remove them. Export your save states to keep a backup.

The first artwork pack replaces only explicitly matched scenes. Game logic, controls, text and unmatched images remain the original game. The player is not a universal automatic 3D remake.
